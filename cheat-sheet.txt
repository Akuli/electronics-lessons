## Important formulas

- Lessons 1 and 2: [`V(A → B) + V(B → C) = V(A → C)`](01#the-important-equation). Describes how voltages from multiple batteries add up, or how one voltage spreads among multiple components. Also essential for understanding how ground works.
- Lesson 3: [`sum(currents in) = sum(currents out)`](03#the-important-equation-of-currents). Applies to basically any component. For two-leg components, this means that you only need to consider one current.
- Lesson 4: [`I = V/R`, or `V = RI`](04#what-is-resistance). This is how a resistor works.
- Lesson 5: [`P = VI`](05#current-of-1-5kw-heater). The power in watts that a power supply outputs, or how much a component heats up.
- Lesson 5: [`P = V^2 / R` and `P = R I^2`](05#power-of-resistor-convenient-equations). These are `P = VI` applied to a resistors.
- Lesson 6: [`current = capacitance * derivative(voltage)`](06#capacitors-and-derivatives). This is how a capacitor works: current flows whenever the voltage changes.
