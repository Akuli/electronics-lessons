title: Electronics Lessons

Hello, and welcome to this site!

I am Akuli, and I'm teaching electronics to my friend adder
with the goal of making adder **understand** electronics very well.
This site contains our chat logs with a bit of cleanup and organization to make them readable.

The unique thing about this site is that you see not only what I'm teaching to adder,
but also what questions adder asked and how I answered them.
This means that we often go slightly off-topic,
and if you're new to electronics, you will likely learn slightly more things than you thought you would learn.
I think this is a good thing.

I don't explain absolutely everything,
because there are some things that me and adder already know very well,
but I try my best to answer anything that adder asks.
For example, adder already had a multimeter when we started, so I didn't really explain what it is.
As such, this site by itself is not enough to get started with electronics.
However, I expect this site to be useful alongside some other intro-to-electronics resource of your choice.
